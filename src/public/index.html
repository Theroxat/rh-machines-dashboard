Which headwear grants which rarity

A Machine's rarity is sealed on chain, and it always matches the Headwear it wears. This table is derived from the collection itself on every boot, not written down — if a headwear ever appeared under two different rarities, it would be named here rather than filed under one of them.

A Machine's rarity — x1 through x10 — is granted by its headwear and sealed at mint. Its weight is rarity multiplied by ink, and weight is what every payout is proportional to: a x6 re-inked to ×2 weighs 12x and takes twelve times a baseline Machine's share of every drop. Those Machines carry an amber badge on their picture, because their row alone would understate them.

The ink ceiling in the header is a third multiplier again: it caps how much extra weight burning more $PRINTER can buy, and it rises as the protocol burns more. A ceiling of x2 does not stop a x10 Machine from existing.

Each cell states two separate numbers: how many Machines are on sale, and how many exist in this class. It shows the eight cheapest and, when it is holding more back, offers them on a button — so a cell with no button is already showing everything it has. A cell reading no listing has nothing on sale; it is not a floor of zero. Hover a cell for its peloton and its exact listed share; hover a Machine for its headwear.
