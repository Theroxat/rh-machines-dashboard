How to tell a Machine's class by looking at it

The class is sealed on chain, but it always matches the Headwear trait. This table is derived from the collection itself on every boot, not written down — if a headwear ever appeared in two classes, it would be named here rather than filed into one of them.

A cell reading no listing has none — it is not a floor of zero. A cell reading no peloton has too few listings for a median to mean anything. Hover any cell for its peloton and exact listed share.
